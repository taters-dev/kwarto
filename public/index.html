Is Kwarto a hotel?

No. Kwarto finds you a room across 22 Philippine destinations, then hands you to Booking.com to finish. We never take your card.

Can I see prices in pesos?

Yes. Prices show in PHP by default. Flip to USD with the toggle in the header. The final checkout currency is set by the partner.

Where does the booking happen?

On Booking.com, with your dates and guests carried over. Cancellation terms are the partner’s and show before you pay.

Which cities are live?

Cebu first. Boracay, Siargao, Bohol, Palawan and the rest of the rail above are city pages; hotel cards appear as soon as we have data for a city.
